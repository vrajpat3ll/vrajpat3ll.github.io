A running collection of observations from building Orion.

## The claim

An agent's reliability comes more from the shape of its tools and loop than
from the length of its instructions. When the loop is explicit — edit, run,
read, decide — failures are localised and recoverable.

## Notes so far

- Fewer tools, each with a clear contract, beat many tools with overlapping
  behaviour.
- Making the result of every action observable keeps the agent grounded.
- The prompt should describe constraints, not procedure.
