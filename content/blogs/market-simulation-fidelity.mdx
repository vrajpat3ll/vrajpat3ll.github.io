The working hypothesis is that a surprising amount of apparent edge in a
reinforcement-learning trading policy is really an artefact of optimistic
fills. This note tracks the experiment that tries to falsify that.

## Setup

Two versions of the same environment: one with idealised fills, one with fees,
slippage and partial fills enabled. The agent, the reward and the seed stay
fixed — only the execution model changes.

## What I'm watching for

- Whether the policy's behaviour changes qualitatively, or just its score.
- Whether the honest environment pushes it toward more conservative positions.

> Draft findings — no numbers yet. The point of writing this down early is to
> commit to a methodology before the results are in.
