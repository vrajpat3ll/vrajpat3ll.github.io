My working hypothesis: much of what looks like edge in a reinforcement-learning
trading policy comes from optimistic fills, not from the strategy. This note
tracks the experiment meant to falsify that.

## Setup

Two versions of the same environment: one with idealised fills, one with fees,
slippage and partial fills enabled. The agent, the reward and the seed stay
fixed. Only the execution model changes.

## What I'm watching for

- Whether the policy's behaviour changes qualitatively, or just its score.
- Whether the realistic environment pushes it toward more conservative positions.

> Draft findings, no numbers yet. Writing this down early commits me to a
> methodology before the results arrive.
